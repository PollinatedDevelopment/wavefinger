Known Bugs

  • On iOS Safari, sound sometimes doesn't play on the very first touch — try tapping the pad again, or open Customize once first.
  • Grid snapping at low grid-size values can feel inconsistent on fast swipes.
  • Multi-finger (chord) playing may occasionally leave a note stuck on until you tap Clear trails or reload.
  • Sustain mode can leave notes ringing indefinitely — use "Clear trails" or "Mute" to stop all sound.
  • Undo only stores the last 15 strokes and clears when you tap "Clear trails."
  • Wind and Organ instruments are more CPU-intensive — may stutter on older devices with many fingers down at once.
