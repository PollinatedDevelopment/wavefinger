Known Bugs

  • On iOS Safari, sound can occasionally need a second touch on the pad to fully kick in.
  • Grid snapping at low grid-size values can feel inconsistent on very fast swipes.
  • Multi-finger playing may rarely leave a note stuck on — tap "Clear" or "Mute" to stop it.
  • Sustain mode holds notes indefinitely by design — remember to Clear or Mute when done.
  • Undo only stores the last 15 strokes, and the history clears when you tap "Clear."
  • Wind and Organ are more CPU-intensive and may stutter on older devices with several fingers down.
  • Rank Road, unlocks, and the legacy theme are saved to this device/browser only — they don't transfer or sync.
  • Radio only plays real uploaded audio files — streaming links (YouTube/Spotify/TikTok) aren't supported.
  • Beat detection reacts to bass spikes, not full musical structure — works best on tracks with a clear, punchy beat.

Radio

Upload an MP3 or other audio file from your device. The pad reacts live to its actual bass, mids, and beat — this only works with a real audio file, not streaming links (YouTube/Spotify/TikTok can't be analyzed this way from a webpage).

When on and a track is playing: the grid pulses with the beat, particle bursts get more intense on bass hits, and the background subtly brightens with the music's energy.
