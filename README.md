# wavefinger
Finger swiping app, version 1.2 out now
swipe your finger to play music. customize your trail, sound, and snap settings.

# 1.2 Patch Notes
Removed volume swipe feature: now wherever you swipe the volume is consistent.

added more sound options such as 8-bit and wind.

added an undo button for your last changes.

your trail no longer fades out so you can draw.

added musical scales, background grid colors, tap explosion effects and line thickness options.

Thanks for playing!
